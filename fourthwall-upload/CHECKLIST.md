# Fourthwall setup checklist

Every file in this folder is a 4096 x 4096 transparent PNG, named for the
product and placement it goes on. Total time: about 45 minutes.

## 1. Shop basics (5 min)
- Dashboard → Settings → General: shop name **Fuck Demetri**.
- Settings → Payouts: connect your bank.
- Settings → Domains: optional, add `shop.fuckdemetri.com` later.

## 2. Products (about 5 min each)
For each row: Products → Add product → pick the blank → Upload design →
drop the file → position → set price → Save.

| # | File | Blank to pick | Placement | Product name | Price |
|---|------|---------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | 01-classic-tee-FRONT.png | Comfort Colors 1717 tee, Black | Front, centered, ~11 in wide | The Classic Tee | $25 |
| 2 | 02-hoodie-FRONT.png | Gildan 18500 or Independent IND4000 hoodie, Black | Front, centered, ~11 in wide | The Hoodie Demetri Will Ask For | $55 |
| 3 | 03-bomber-or-zip-hoodie-LEFT-CHEST.png | Zip hoodie, Black (Fourthwall has no bomber) | Left chest, ~3.5 in wide | The Bomber (rename to "The Zip" if you swap) | $85 → suggest $65 for a zip hoodie |
| 4 | 04-fd-hoodie-LEFT-CHEST.png | Same hoodie as #2, Black | Left chest, ~3.5 in wide | FD Hoodie | $55 |
| 5 | 05-fd-tee-LEFT-CHEST.png | Same tee as #1, Black | Left chest, ~3.5 in wide | FD Tee | $25 |
| 6 | 06-dad-hat-FRONT-embroidery.png | Dad hat / unstructured cap, Black, **embroidery** | Front center, ~2.5 in wide | Dad Hat | $22 |
| 7 | 07-sticker-DIE-CUT.png | Die-cut sticker, 3 in | Full | Sticker Pack (set quantity 3 or sell singles) | $8 |
| 8 | 08-mug-WRAP.png | 11 oz black mug | Right side (or both sides) | The Mug | $18 |

Paste these descriptions:

1. Black tee, full two-color lockup on the chest. Says exactly what you think it says.
2. Heavyweight. Comfortable. Legally distinct from an apology.
3. Small FD monogram on the chest. For people who want plausible deniability.
4. Same hoodie, quieter. Just the monogram. You know what it stands for.
5. Black tee with the FD chest hit. Subtle, until someone asks.
6. FD monogram embroidered on the front. For sunny days and bad decisions.
7. Three black FD stickers, die-cut. Laptops, water bottles, Demetri's car.
8. Holds coffee. Holds grudges.

Notes:
- Fourthwall shows "profit" per item on the price screen. Aim for at least $10 on tees, $25 on hoodies.
- For embroidery (#6) Fourthwall may ask you to confirm thread colors. Red for the F, off-white/cream for the D.
- The `alt-*-white-one-color.png` files are for any blank where two colors cost extra.

## 3. Launch (2 min)
- Dashboard → Publish shop.
- Copy the shop URL (looks like `fuck-demetri-shop.fourthwall.com`).
- Send it to me, or paste it into `shopUrl` in `site.config.js`. Every merch
  button on the website will then go straight to the shop.

## 4. Before telling anyone (optional but smart)
- Order one tee and one hoodie for yourself to check print quality and sizing.
